kedarnath

Kedarnath is a holy Hindu town and pilgrimage center in Uttarakhand, India. It sits at an altitude of 3,583 meters (11,755 feet) near the Mandakini River. Famous for the ancient Kedarnath Temple dedicated to Lord Shiva, it is one of the twelve sacred Jyotirlingas and part of the Char Dham yatra.

gangotri

Gangotri is a town in Uttarakhand, India, known for the Gangotri Temple dedicated to Goddess Ganga. It is the origin of the Ganges River and a significant pilgrimage site for Hindus. The town is surrounded by snow-capped mountains and glaciers, making it a popular destination for trekkers and spiritual seekers.

yamanotri

Yamunotri is a town in Uttarakhand, India, known for the Yamunotri Temple dedicated to Goddess Yamuna. It is the source of the Yamuna River and a significant pilgrimage site for Hindus. The town is surrounded by the Himalayas and is accessible via trekking routes, attracting devotees and adventure enthusiasts alike.
